Privacy Policy

At Insurance Path, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or explore our insurance services.

01

Introduction

Welcome to Insurance Path. We understand the importance of protecting your personal information and maintaining your trust.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared with third parties, and the choices available to you regarding your personal information.

Whether you are exploring insurance solutions, requesting a quote, or contacting our team for assistance, we aim to handle your information with care, transparency, and responsibility.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information depending on how you interact with our website and services.

Personal Information

We may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • ZIP code or location information
  • Date of birth, where required
  • Other contact details you voluntarily provide

Insurance-Related Information

  • Type of insurance service requested
  • Desired coverage and protection requirements
  • Vehicle and property-related information
  • Travel and business-related details

Technical Information

  • IP address and browser information
  • Device type and operating system
  • Website pages visited and browsing activity
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide a convenient website experience and respond to your insurance requirements.

  • Process and respond to quote requests and enquiries.
  • Understand your insurance needs and coverage preferences.
  • Connect you with relevant insurance providers, where applicable.
  • Communicate updates regarding your submitted requests.
  • Provide customer support and assistance.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraudulent activities.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.
04

Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not share personal information indiscriminately. Information may be shared when necessary to fulfill your requests or operate our services.

Insurance Providers

Relevant enquiry details may be shared with insurance companies, agencies, or authorized representatives to address your requirements.

Service Partners

We may work with third-party organizations that support website hosting, communications, analytics, and technical services.

Legal Authorities

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable laws, regulations, or legal proceedings.

05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand visitor activity, and enhance your browsing experience.

  • Remember user preferences and settings.
  • Understand website interactions.
  • Analyze website traffic and performance.
  • Improve navigation and functionality.
  • Support website security.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

06

Data Security

Protecting your personal information is important to us. Insurance Path takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard information collected through our website.

Our security practices are intended to help prevent unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss of personal information.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee complete security.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The retention period may depend on:

  • The nature of your enquiry or request.
  • The purpose for which information was collected.
  • Applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Business recordkeeping requirements.
  • The need to resolve disputes.
08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request information about how your data is used.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.

To exercise your applicable privacy rights, please contact our team using the information provided on this website.

09

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, insurance providers, or other external resources.

These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before sharing personal information or using their services.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intentionally directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised Last Updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this page regularly to stay informed about how their personal information is collected, used, and protected.

12

Contact Us

We value your trust and are committed to addressing your privacy-related questions and concerns.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need further information about our data-handling practices, please contact us.
