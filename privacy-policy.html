Your Privacy Is Important to Us

At DSP Comparison, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and services.

01

Introduction

Welcome to DSP Comparison. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you visit our website, explore insurance options, or submit a quote request.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Residential address and ZIP code
  • Date of birth, where required

Insurance-Related Information

  • Insurance category and coverage requirements
  • Vehicle or property details
  • Travel or business-related information
  • Other details submitted through our forms

Technical Information

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device and operating system information
  • Website usage and browsing activity
  • Cookies and tracking information
03

How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide relevant insurance comparison options.
  • Process and respond to quote requests.
  • Connect you with relevant insurance providers.
  • Respond to questions and provide support.
  • Improve our website and user experience.
  • Send service-related updates.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities.
04

Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with insurance companies, authorized representatives, technology providers, and other service partners when necessary to provide our services.

When you submit an insurance quote request, your information may be shared with relevant providers so they can respond to your requirements.

05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand website traffic, analyze user behavior, and improve website performance.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with applicable legal requirements, and resolve disputes.

08

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.

Our website may contain links to external websites, including insurance providers. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or security policies.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization where required by law.

11

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business practices, website functionality, or applicable legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page.
