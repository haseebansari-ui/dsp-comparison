Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform, insurance resources, and home improvement services.

What services are available on this website?

Our platform provides access to information about life, health, auto, home, travel, and business insurance, along with home improvement services such as roofing, HVAC, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, and solar installation.

How can I get started?

You can browse the service categories, select the service you're interested in, and submit an enquiry form with your relevant details.

Do you provide insurance policies directly?

Our platform helps users discover insurance information and relevant resources. Insurance policies, eligibility, coverage, and pricing are determined by the relevant insurer or authorized provider.

Can I submit an enquiry for home improvement services?

Yes. You can share your project requirements through the relevant enquiry form. We may help direct your request to suitable service providers, depending on location and availability.

Is there a fee for using the website?

Browsing our website and submitting an enquiry may be free. However, insurance premiums, consultation charges, and service provider fees may apply.

How will my information be used?

Your information may be used to process your enquiry, respond to your request, and connect you with relevant providers where applicable. Please review our Privacy Policy for more details.

How can I contact your team?

You can reach us through the Contact Us page or by using the official contact details listed on our website.
