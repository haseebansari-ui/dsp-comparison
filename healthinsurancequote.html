UNDERSTANDING HEALTH INSURANCE
What Is Health Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

Health insurance covers eligible medical costs, including hospitalization and essential procedures, according to your policy terms.

It protects your personal savings from the rising cost of medical treatments during healthcare emergencies.

When choosing a plan, consider your family size, network hospitals, coverage limits, waiting periods, and premium affordability.

Benefits of Health Insurance

  • Eligible hospital expenses are covered according to your policy's benefits, limits, and conditions.
  • Covers eligible medical expenses before and after hospitalization for specified timeframes.
  • Allows multiple eligible family members to share a common sum insured under a single policy.
  • Access eligible care through an insurer's network hospitals, subject to authorization and terms.
  • Reduces the financial impact of covered medical emergencies and guards your personal savings.
  • Premiums may qualify for tax deductions under Section 80D of applicable Indian tax laws.
  • Includes specified diagnostic services, treatments, and outpatient medical care per plan terms.
  • Compare individual and family plans easily based on coverage limits, premiums, and policy features.
