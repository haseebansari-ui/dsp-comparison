UNDERSTANDING HOME INSURANCE
What Is Home Insurance and Why Does It Matter?

Home insurance helps safeguard your home and personal contents against unexpected damage or loss according to policy terms.

Protection for Your Home

Home insurance safeguards your structural investment against major risks like fire and severe storms per policy terms.

Coverage for Your Belongings

Protects valuable personal belongings like furniture, electronics, appliances, and clothing against covered damages.

Choose Coverage That Fits Your Needs

Compare coverage based on property type, location, rebuild costs, belongings, deductibles, and exclusions.

Benefits of Home Insurance

  • Explore coverage for eligible damage to the insured building, subject to policy terms and limits.
  • Find policies that may protect furniture, electronics, appliances, and other covered possessions.
  • Review coverage for specified events such as fire, storms, or other insured perils.
  • Check whether theft or burglary losses are covered and what conditions or limits apply.
  • Insurance may reduce the financial burden of certain covered losses, according to the policy.
  • Some policies include additional living expenses when a covered event makes a home temporarily uninhabitable.
  • Certain policies offer liability protection for eligible claims involving injury to others or damage to their property.
  • Compare limits, deductibles, exclusions, and optional coverages to find an option that fits your circumstances.
