UNDERSTANDING AUTO INSURANCE
What Is Auto Insurance and Why Is It Important?

Auto insurance protects vehicle owners from financial risks caused by accidents, theft, and covered damages, offering third-party liability and optional own-damage coverage.

In India, third-party motor insurance is mandatory for public roads, while comprehensive policies provide broader financial protection by covering both third-party liabilities and your own vehicle's damages.

Selecting the right plan depends on your vehicle type, usage, insured declared value (IDV), and budget. Comparing policy limits, premiums, add-ons, and exclusions ensures an informed choice.

Benefits of Auto Insurance

  • Covers legal liability for third-party injury, death, or property damage per policy terms and law.
  • Assists with repair or replacement costs following covered accidents and specified events.
  • Provides coverage if your insured vehicle is stolen according to policy conditions.
  • Protects against vehicle damage caused by floods, storms, and specified natural events.
  • Access hassle-free repairs at authorized network garages subject to approval and deductibles.
  • Earn discounts on own-damage premiums for claim-free policy years.
  • Customize protection with zero depreciation, roadside assistance, and engine protection coverage.
  • Evaluate premiums, Insured Declared Value (IDV), deductibles, add-ons, and exclusions before choosing.
