UNDERSTANDING TRAVEL INSURANCE
What Is Travel Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

Travel insurance safeguards your journey against unexpected costs from medical emergencies, trip cancellations, delays, and lost luggage.

Protection During Medical Emergencies

Covers eligible medical expenses and urgent care incurred due to unexpected illness or injury while traveling.

Support When Travel Plans Change

Reimburses eligible expenses caused by covered flight cancellations, travel delays, and unexpected interruptions.

Travel With Greater Peace of Mind

Compare limits, exclusions, destinations, and benefits to find the right protection for solo, family, or business trips.

Benefits of Travel Insurance

  • Explore protection for eligible medical treatment and emergency healthcare expenses while travelling.
  • Certain policies may reimburse eligible prepaid travel expenses when a trip is cancelled for a covered reason.
  • Find options that may help cover eligible additional expenses caused by covered travel delays.
  • Explore coverage for eligible baggage loss, theft, or delayed baggage during your journey.
  • Some policies provide assistance or coverage for eligible emergency transportation to an appropriate medical facility.
  • Certain plans may include cover for eligible third-party liability claims arising during your trip.
  • Explore plans designed for families travelling together, with benefits and eligibility depending on the policy.
  • Some policies offer support services for travellers who need assistance while away from home.
